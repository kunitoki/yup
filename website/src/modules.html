modules

Take only what you need. Each yup_* module is a self-contained unity build with its own declaration: list the ones you use and the CMake API resolves the rest.

01CoreFoundations used across the whole framework.

  • yup_coresource

    Strings, containers, files and streams, memory, math, time, threading, networking and data interchange. No YUP dependencies.

    needs zlib

  • yup_eventssource

    The message thread, timers, async callbacks and inter-object notifications behind every interactive app.

    needs yup_core

  • yup_simdsource

    Vectorized math across SSE, AVX, FMA, NEON and Accelerate, wrapping xsimd behind a YUP-friendly API.

    needs yup_core, xsimd

02DataObservable application state.

  • yup_data_modelsource

    The DataTree model: hierarchical, observable, transactional and schema-validatable data with an XPath-like query engine.

    needs yup_events

03AudioDevices, formats, DSP, processing graphs and plugins.

  • yup_audio_basicssource

    Audio buffers, MIDI messages, synthesis helpers and processing utilities.

    needs yup_core, yup_simd

  • yup_audio_devicessource

    Realtime audio and MIDI I/O: enumerate, open and stream from platform devices.

    needs yup_audio_basics, yup_events

  • yup_audio_formatssource

    Read and write Wav, Mp3, OGG, Flac, Opus and more, enabling each codec by linking its library.

    needs yup_audio_basics, yup_simd

  • yup_dspsource

    Filters and filter designers, crossovers and spectral analysis, with optional fast FFTs and time-stretching.

    needs yup_core, yup_audio_basics, yup_simd

  • yup_dsp_jitsource

    YDSP: a realtime JIT-compiled DSP language compiling patches to native x86-64 and AArch64 code that runs allocation-free in the audio callback.

    needs yup_core, yup_dsp, yup_audio_basics

  • yup_audio_processorssource

    The AudioProcessor model that plugins and the audio graph are built from, plus parameter handling.

    needs yup_audio_basics, yup_data_model, yup_dsp

  • yup_audio_graphsource

    A node-based graph of AudioProcessors for routing audio and MIDI, backed by the data model.

    needs yup_audio_processors, yup_data_model

  • yup_audio_plugin_clientsource

    Wrap your own AudioProcessor as a CLAP, VST3 or AU plugin, or as a standalone app.

    needs yup_audio_processors, yup_gui

  • yup_audio_plugin_hostsource

    In-process hosting of third-party VST3, CLAP, LV2 and AU plugins.

    needs yup_audio_processors

04GraphicsThe rendering stack, from GPU abstraction to 2D drawing and animation.

  • yup_rhisource

    Low-level GPU abstraction: devices, compute and render pipelines, buffers, textures and offscreen targets, with everything needed to render 3D into components and components into 3D. Use it for GPU compute without the 2D stack.

    needs yup_core, yup_shading, yup_simd, rive

  • yup_shadingsource

    Shader-source containers, a cross-platform shader transpiler and the .ysl shader-bundle format consumed by the RHI, for offline or runtime compilation.

    needs yup_core

  • yup_graphicssource

    2D drawing and windowed rendering on the RHI and Rive: paths, fonts, SVG, imaging and offscreen GPU canvases.

    needs yup_core, yup_simd, yup_rhi, yup_shading, rive, libclipper2

  • yup_animationsource

    A Lottie-compatible animation model with playback, rendering and export.

    needs yup_core, yup_graphics

05UIComponents and widgets painted on the GPU.

  • yup_guisource

    Components, windowing, events, layout and widgets that paint through the graphics stack and bind to the data model.

    needs yup_events, yup_data_model, yup_graphics, rive

  • yup_audio_guisource

    Waveforms, meters, spectrum analyzers and audio-graph editor views bridging the UI and audio stacks.

    needs yup_audio_basics, yup_audio_devices, yup_audio_formats, yup_audio_processors, yup_audio_graph, yup_dsp, yup_gui

06ScriptingDrive YUP from Python.

  • yup_pythonsource

    Python bindings for creating and driving YUP applications, generated for every supported module present in the build.

    needs yup_core

07AILLM clients, embeddings and the Model Context Protocol.

  • yup_aisource

    Chat-completion clients for OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini, function-calling tools, embeddings and an MCP client and server.

    needs yup_core, yup_events
